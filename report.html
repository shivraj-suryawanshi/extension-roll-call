How the rating works

The score adds up three things: which websites an extension can run on, which browser features it can use, and where it was installed from. 0 to 14 is Low, 15 to 34 Medium, 35 to 59 High, and 60 or more Critical.

A high rating means powerful access, not that the extension is harmful. A password manager needs wide access to do its job. Use the rating to decide which extensions to look at first.
